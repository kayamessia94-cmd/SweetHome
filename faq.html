Frequently asked questions

What is Sweet Home?

Sweet Home is a house hunting company. We help people find homes to rent or buy, and also hotels and restaurants nearby.

Who is Sweet Home for?

For everyone, especially students who are not used to searching for a place to live.

How do I find a house to rent?

Open the Rent a Home section on the home page, browse the available houses, then contact us with the one you like.

Can I sell my house through Sweet Home?

Yes. Use the Sell a House section or send us a message with details about your property, and we will help you reach buyers.

Does it cost anything to use the website?

Browsing is free. Contact us for details about fees on specific services.

How can I find a hotel or restaurant near me?

Use the Hotel Nearby and Restaurant Map services from the Services menu.
