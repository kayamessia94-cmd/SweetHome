About US

Our Story

Sweet Home began as a school project built around a simple idea: making it easier for people to find the places they need. What started as an academic assignment gradually became an opportunity to imagine something bigger — a platform that could make finding a home simpler for everyone.

Make property searches easier.

Sweet Home is a platform designed to connect people with properties and places that match their needs. From renting and buying homes to discovering hotels and restaurants, we aim to make every search more convenient, accessible, and reliable.

Conneting people with the right places.

We believe every great journey begins with the right place. Our ambition is to turn the search for a property into a simpler, more meaningful experience — helping people find not just spaces, but places where life happens and memories begin.

Making every place feel like home.
